Redback upholds strict standards of technical authenticity. Verifiable credentials are respected by hiring companies because they validate authentic hands-on competence.

warning Zero Tolerance for Plagiarism Submitting third-party source code without proper attribution, copying project solutions from peer interns, employing automated bots to spoof module completion, or fabricating project URLs will result in immediate disqualification and revocation of any issued certificates.

Redback reserves the right to audit task submissions at any time before or after issuing an offer letter or completion certificate.

Verified Certificates & Public Verification

Upon successful completion of all mandatory course modules, interns become eligible to receive an official Certificate of Completion.

  • Unique Credentials: Every certificate carries a unique tamper-proof Certificate ID (e.g., CRT-XXXXXXXXXX) and Intern ID (e.g., RBS/INT/XXXXXX).
  • Instant QR Verification: All certificates feature a secure QR code that resolves directly to the official Redback Public Verification Portal (Verify Portal).
  • Third-Party Employer Checks: Potential employers, colleges, and university placement cells may publicly look up your credential to verify authenticity, domain course, completion status, and issue date.
  • Revocation: Redback reserves the right to cancel or revoke any certificate found to have been obtained through fraud, identity impersonation, or academic dishonesty.

Redback offers both Free and Certified internship courses:

  • Free Courses: When a course is designated as FREE, interns may complete task modules and unlock their verified certificate at no cost (₹0).
