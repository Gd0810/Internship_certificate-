Our Core Privacy Pledge

Redback never sells, rents, or monetizes student data to third-party advertisers or lead-generation brokers. Your information is used exclusively to facilitate your practical internship learning, issue verifiable certificates, and confirm authentic completion records to authorized recruiters upon your request.

1

Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected by Redback IT Solutions Private Limited through our internship platform, task dashboards, public certificate verification tools, and communications channels.

By accessing our platform or enrolling in any internship course, you consent to the data collection and usage practices described in this policy.

2

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly, as well as data generated automatically during your course of study:

badge Personal & Profile Data

Full Name, Email Address, Contact Number, Academic Institution (College/University), Degree / Major, and District of Residence.

assignment Academic & Deliverables Data

Selected Internship Course, module progression logs, submitted code links, project notes, feedback remarks, and completion timestamps.

verified Credential & ID Identifiers

Unique Intern ID (RBS/INT/XXXXXX), Certificate ID (CRT-XXXXXXXXXX), and verification QR code URLs.

devices Technical & Log Information

IP address, browser type, device operating system, login session tokens, and security access audit logs.

3

How We Use Your Information

We process your data for legitimate educational and platform administration purposes:

  • Administering Internships: Processing enrollment, assigning task modules, and updating your progress percentages in real time.
  • Official Offer Letters & Certificates: Rendering personalized, verifiable PDF credentials bearing your legal name, course title, and unique tracking code.
  • Communications: Sending account confirmations, module reviews, certificate unlock notifications, and customer support responses.
  • Academic Integrity & Security: Detecting and mitigating plagiarism, account hijacking, multiple account abuse, or falsified submissions.
  • Platform Optimization: Analyzing aggregate completion metrics to refine course curriculum and improve usability.
4

Public Certificate Verification Portal

A cornerstone of the Redback platform is employer-verifiable certification. To prevent credential forgery, our platform provides a public lookup portal accessible via:

  • Scanning the QR code printed on your official Certificate of Completion.
  • Entering your unique Intern ID or Certificate ID at redback.in/verify.
What is displayed on the public verification page:
check Intern Full Name
check Internship Domain Course
check Official Intern ID
check Completion Status
check Certificate Issue Date
check Verifiable Credential ID

Note: Sensitive details such as your password hash, email address, mobile number, and payment transaction details are never displayed on the public verification page.

5

Payment & Gateway Security

When you pay for a premium certificate verification fee:

  • Transactions are processed securely through certified, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (e.g. Razorpay).
  • Redback does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card PINs, CVV codes, or net banking passwords.
  • We only retain non-sensitive transaction metadata (Order ID, Payment ID, Amount, Timestamp, and Payment Status) to reconcile receipts and unlock your certificate.
6

Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Hiring Partners & Employers: When a hiring partner or corporate recruiter queries our certificate verification portal using your credential ID to confirm authentic completion.
  • Academic Institutions: If you enrolled through an institutional college MoU or university partnership, aggregate or verified completion rosters may be shared with your college placement cell.
  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party cloud hosting, transactional email delivery (e.g., SMTP services), and payment processors operating under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by Indian law enforcement, court subpoenas, or regulatory authorities to protect against fraud or platform safety threats.
7

Cookies & Session Management

We use strictly necessary and functional cookies to ensure a secure session:

  • Authentication Cookies: To keep you securely logged into your intern dashboard across page reloads.
  • CSRF Security Tokens: To protect forms against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks.
  • Preference Cookies: To remember UI state, active filter tabs, or search queries.

We do not use invasive cross-site ad trackers or behavioral profiling cookies.

8

Data Security Safeguards

We apply multi-layered technical and administrative controls to protect your records:

  • Encryption in Transit: 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption across all endpoints and web pages.
  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: User passwords are encrypted using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and never stored in plain text.
  • Access Control: Administrative access to student data is restricted to authorized personnel with role-based permissions and audit logs.
9

Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain student registration records and task progress for as long as your account remains active. Verified completion certificates and associated credential IDs are retained indefinitely to guarantee lifetime verification for your future employers.

You may request deletion of non-essential profile data or account deactivation by contacting our privacy team at info@redback.in.

10

Your Privacy Rights

In accordance with applicable privacy regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access and review the personal information associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information (e.g. spelling errors in name, college, or district).
  • Request a copy of your task evaluation logs and certificate records.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.
11

Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological advancements, legal requirements, or platform updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". Continued use of Redback after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
